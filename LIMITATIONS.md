# CropGuard AI: Technical Limitations & Prototype Disclosures

This document provides transparent, honest disclosures regarding the scope, technical boundaries, and operational constraints of the CropGuard AI working prototype.

---

## 1. Machine Learning Dataset & Scope

- **Class Coverage**: The prototype CNN (MobileNetV3) is trained on a curated 5-class dataset:
  1. `Tomato_Early_Blight` (*Alternaria solani*)
  2. `Tomato_Late_Blight` (*Phytophthora infestans*)
  3. `Potato_Early_Blight` (*Alternaria solani*)
  4. `Corn_Common_Rust` (*Puccinia sorghi*)
  5. `Healthy_Leaf` (Multiple species baseline)
- **Domain Shift**: While image synthesis and augmentation techniques were applied to mimic field conditions, real-world deployment across diverse Indian agro-climatic zones requires training on multi-thousand field image datasets (e.g., PlantDoc + localized KVK field photos) to handle complex backdrops, weed contamination, and leaf shadowing.

---

## 2. Stage A Epidemiological Risk Model (Proof-of-Concept)

- **POC Methodology**: The Stage A pre-symptomatic forecasting engine demonstrates the **Van der Plank Disease Triangle** methodology using micro-climate variables (Temperature, Relative Humidity, Rainfall, Leaf Wetness Duration) combined with crop growth stage sensitivity.
- **Agronomic Calibration**: In this hackathon/prototype build, the parameters use simplified biophysical thresholds and a logistic regression model trained on synthetic weather correlation data. It serves as a proof-of-concept for pre-symptomatic risk pushing, rather than a multi-year calibrated agronomic forecasting tool.

---

## 3. Data Ingestion & External API Dependencies

- **Weather Data**: Integrated with synthetic/open-meteo micro-climate generation. In production, this would be wired directly to live India Meteorological Department (IMD) / Agromet automatic weather stations (AWS).
- **Advisory Content**: Treatment recommendations are paraphrased from publicly available ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) and State Agricultural University (SAU) advisory packages, compiled into an internal SQLite database (`cropguard.db`). There is no live government API hookup.

---

## 4. Optical & Diagnostic Caveats

- **Image Quality**: Accuracy depends on photo clarity, lighting, and frame filling. Extremely blurry images or photos taken from a long distance may yield reduced confidence.
- **Severity Quantification**: % leaf area affected is calculated using HSV color space lesion segmentation. Extreme specular reflections (sun glare) or insect chew marks may introduce slight variances in calculated lesion area.
- **Regulatory & Safety Disclaimer**: All treatment advisories (organic and chemical) generated by the system include a strict disclaimer: *"Treatments are provided for guidance only. Consult your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Block Agriculture Officer for exact chemical dosage and regional tank-mix recommendations."*
